# 欠債喻言（1876 年北京刊，牛津大學 Sinica 2036） / Qian zhai yu yan [Parable of the Debt]: Transcription Dataset

《欠債喻言》是 1876 年由北京燈市口美華書院刷印的官話小冊，Serica 館目署 M. B. North。故事用國王、城中欠債居民和代為償債的太子解說基督教的赦免與救贖，並附經文欄注及四節聖歌。牛津大學博德利圖書館 Sinica 2036 底本可供近代官話、宗教出版及圖文版式研究。

Qian zhai yu yan is an illustrated Mandarin Christian tract printed in 1876 at the American Presbyterian Mission Press on Dengshikou in Beijing and attributed to M. B. North in the Serica catalogue. It tells of a king, a city of debtors and a prince who pays their debts, with marginal scripture quotations and a four-stanza hymn. The transcription follows Bodleian Libraries Sinica 2036.

## V0.1.0

13 幅共 24 面，含封面、卷前經文、正文原頁碼一至二十、無題聖歌與後封。正文插圖及後封花籃圖有位置。AI 輔助逐欄初錄，疑讀另記。

The 13 scans represent 24 faces: title page, opening scripture quotations, pages 1–20, an untitled four-stanza hymn and the back cover. An illustration in the text and the back-cover ornament are located. AI-assisted initial transcription with uncertain readings marked.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。正文旁的經文、印刷點、人物名線、頁碼、聖歌上下段及館藏附記分層記錄。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Marginal verses, punctuation, name lines, printed pagination, the hymn’s two registers and copy annotations are represented separately. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
